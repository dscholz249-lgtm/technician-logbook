Product Overview

Log it by text. See it in the dashboard.

SkillCat Logbook lets field managers and technicians log training activity, photos, and notes by texting a single number — no app download, no login required in the field. Everything surfaces in a web dashboard for managers and directors.

How it works
Step 01
Manager gets set up
Manager receives a magic link to the web portal and saves their phone number in Settings. That phone number is how the system knows who they are when they text.
Step 02
Technicians are invited
Manager invites technicians via email from the Technicians page. Techs click their magic link and can optionally link their phone by texting their email address to the SkillCat number.
Step 03
Everyone logs by text
Managers text training assignments, notes, and photos. Technicians text updates and job site photos. Everything is captured in the dashboard automatically.

Sales & CS Tips
The system understands natural language — managers don't need to memorize exact formats. "Put Mike on the OSHA course" works just as well as "Assign Mike to OSHA 30."
To start logging by SMS, managers (and techs) just text their registered email address to the SkillCat number. They get a confirmation email, click "Yes, this is me," and they're linked — no portal login required. Settings still has a manual phone field as a fallback.
Technicians don't need to do anything to get started — their manager can start logging notes about them immediately from the portal.
Phone linking for technicians is optional. Without it, managers log on their behalf. With it, techs can log their own updates directly.
Directors see the whole company; managers see their own activity only. If a customer has multiple locations, they likely need a director account.
If the system doesn't understand a message, it asks a clarifying question. Unresolved requests go to a human review queue for the SkillCat team.
